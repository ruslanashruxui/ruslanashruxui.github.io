how I work

RESEARCH FIRST,
THEN PIXELS.

I like to understand a product before I design it: what the market already does, what people need, and why each decision is made.

to-do: every project

  • RESEARCH BEFORE SCREENS

    I go deep into competitors — sign up for their products and map what the market already does — before drawing anything.

  • DECISIONS WITH A WHY

    Every flow comes with reasons: what I saw, what I decided, and how it changes the experience.

  • FEWER STEPS, MORE RESULT

    my favourite ♡

    My rule for every flow: the fewest actions for the biggest result. If a step doesn't earn its place, it goes.

  • AI AS A WORKING TOOL

    ChatGPT, Claude and Figma Make help me explore faster. I was the first at my studio to bring Figma Make into project work.

about me

HI, I'M RUSLANA.

I like things to make sense — and to feel good. I’ve worked as the only designer next to a PM, in a designer pair, and as designer + PM leading a client on my own — from wireframes to handoff.

I notice small things: a tone that feels off, a step that's one too many, a detail that makes a screen feel calm. Behind every interface I try to see the person who will use it.
