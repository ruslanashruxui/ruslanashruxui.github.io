01 — ABOUT THE PRODUCT

Protecting ads from invalid clicks

The SaaS analyses ad campaign traffic, filters out bots and suspicious clicks and blocks them, so the budget is spent on real people. My task was a UI redesign of the whole platform: flows and logic stayed the same — I reworked the visuals, components and states.

WHAT THE PRODUCT DOES

Shows traffic quality, blocks invalid clicks, keeps blacklists and reports by channel and campaign.

WHO IT’S FOR

Marketers and agencies running paid ads across several ad networks.

THE TASK

Refresh the look of the whole platform — and later, at the client’s request, tone down the interface colours.

02 — MY ROLE

The whole UI redesign was mine

There were three of us: me, a team lead and a manager. We joined client calls as a team. I finished the onboarding based on the existing design, and redesigned the rest of the platform — from the dashboard to settings — on my own.

03 — KEY DECISION: COLOUR

Hearing what people want, even when it isn’t spelled out

Before us, the product had a bright palette. When our studio joined, the brand got new colours — dark navy, lavender and lime. Later, the client’s leadership wanted to tone down the colours on the platform itself. I quickly understood what exactly they meant — and the client adopted my colour scheme

06 — REFLECTION

What I took away from this project

Hearing the request behind the words

“Tone down the colours” can mean a dozen things. Instead of debating the wording, I put together 5 theme sets — and the client saw what they wanted. Options on screen convince better than explanations.

A redesign that doesn’t break the logic

The flows and the design system already existed. My job was to make 9 sections feel like one product, without making users relearn anything.

What I’d do differently

Now I’d start with an inventory: collect every table, state and widget across all sections before redesigning the first screen. Consistency is cheaper to plan than to fix later.
