06 — LIVE STREAMING & AUCTIONS

Live auctions: decisions in seconds

CHALLENGE

A live auction is money in real time. Bids change every second, and users always need to understand their status, react fast and not get lost when something goes wrong.

01

Bid status always visible

Highest bidder, Outbid, Bid extended, Won: each state has its own look and one primary action. Outbid? The Re-bid button is right there.

02

Bid before the start

Pre-bid lets users bid on a lot before the stream starts, then edit or cancel it without waiting for the broadcast.

03

Verification without losing context

If KYC or a payment method is missing, the flow leads to verification straight from the action (bid, purchase, chat) and returns the user to the lot.

04

Failures are part of the scenario

Reconnecting, auction paused when the seller drops, unexpected stream end — designed separately for the winner and for viewers.

07 — STREAM SETUP & SCHEDULING

Stream setup: 4 steps instead of chaos

CHALLENGE

Sellers need to prepare a broadcast fast: describe the stream, choose who can see it, add lots and configure how each one sells. A setup mistake means money lost live on air.

01

Step-by-step flow with drafts

Details → Access → Lots → Review. Progress is visible at every step, and a draft can be saved at any time.

02

Access levels

Community, Curated, Trusted, Exclusive and custom access styles like VIP: the seller decides who sees the stream.

03

No going live half-configured

Until a sale type (Auction or Buy Now) is set for every lot, the flow highlights the issue and blocks the next step.

04

Limits for new sellers

Dedicated states for when the lot limit is reached or exceeded, plus an explanation of how limits work.

08 — RIP & SHIP

Rip & Ship: trust on live air

CHALLENGE

A new format for the platform: buyers purchase spots in sealed boosters before the stream, and the seller opens them live. Trust is everything here — buyers must be sure they get exactly the cards pulled from their pack.

01

Explain the format before the first action

A short “How it works” onboarding for sellers and an explainer block for buyers right on the session page.

02

Transparent purchase window

A timer, sold out and window closed states, and reminders: buyers know exactly until when they can join.

03

Rip checklist and AI scanning

The seller shows the sealed box, and every card is scanned inside a frame. All viewers see the “3 of 10 scanned” counter.

04

What to do when something goes wrong

Card count mismatch, lost connection, a rescheduled rip: each case has its own state and a clear next step.

08.1 — KEY DECISION · RIP & SHIP

When one new requirement changes the whole flow

CHALLENGE

Rip & Ship was designed around single packs. Mid-project, boxes and cases were added — and the first idea was to treat a case as just another product card.

01

The challenge

Rip & Ship started with single packs. Then the client added boxes and cases — and suggested treating a case as just another product card.

02

Hidden complexity

A case holds boxes, a box holds packs, a pack holds cards. Each level changes the queue, rip progress, source checks and what the buyer actually receives.

03

Our approach

Together with my teammate I mapped how nesting affects buyer and seller flows, and we showed the client that a case is not a new product card but a different opening, tracking and verification process.

04

Decision

Keep one shared rip pattern for every level and add dedicated steps only where nesting needs them: source case and box checks, next box from case, per-pack progress.

08.2 — KEY DECISION · PORTFOLIO

A pulled card is just an Asset — with a source

CHALLENGE

Cards pulled in a live rip needed a home after the session. A new asset type would have meant new screens, statuses and rules — a parallel system next to the Portfolio.

DECISION

A pulled card joins the Portfolio as a regular Asset that keeps a link to the session it came from. Same statuses and the same actions — selling, grading, storage.

09 — PROFILE, REPUTATION & ACCOUNT HUB

Profile: reputation as currency

CHALLENGE

In a marketplace between strangers, the key question is whether you can trust this seller. The profile has to answer it in seconds, and the Account Hub lets users manage everything from verification to orders.

01

Trust from the first screen

Rating, number of sales and average shipping time — right under the avatar.

02

Account health and levels

Good, Warning and Ban states for sellers and buyers, plus seller level progress: it’s clear where you are and what to do next.

03

Different profile views

Own and public, seller and buyer, new and unverified accounts: each shows exactly what’s needed.

04

From individual to organization

Account type change, business verification, changing the representative and VAT details — step-by-step flows with review states.

I worked with it daily — assembling modules from components, keeping things consistent and updating components and tokens so development received a cohesive UI.
