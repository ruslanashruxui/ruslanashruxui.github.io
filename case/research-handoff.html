03 — ESTIMATES

An estimate is a range, not a single number

I rate each task’s complexity from 1 to 5 and give minimum and maximum hours. In the description I explain what can be reused and what we build from scratch — so the client sees where the number comes from.

AN EXCERPT FROM ONE OF MY ESTIMATES

Auth & onboarding2612The foundation exists: check the flows, add first-login logic, empty states and a mini-onboarding if needed.
Home & main navigation51224—

Complexity separate from hours

Points show risk and uncertainty, hours show the amount of work.

A range instead of a point

Min / max honestly shows what the final number depends on.

Description = agreement

What the task includes and what we reuse — so there are no surprises.

05 — REFLECTION

Design is more than screens

Process can be designed too

I built the Tutor IQ research as a template, not a one-off document — so Flipity’s research was done with it, and it became the basis for research at the studio.

A gap in a mockup is cheaper than a bug

There were no PM specs yet, so I took the review on myself. The main lesson: a gap is easiest to close while it’s still in the mockup — and once it has a type and a specific action.

What I’d do differently

Now I’d check states as I design, not as a separate stage at the end: a short checklist per module would cut the number of gaps before the audit.
